Privacy Policy

Mock Shift – com.locationspoofer.app
Last updated: March 2026

Mock Shift ("the App", "we", "our") is an Android application that provides mock GPS location functionality using Android's official mock location APIs. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and your rights. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • No user accounts, login, or registration.
  • No personal information such as your name, phone number, or email address.
  • No server or backend — your data is never sent to us.
  • All settings, favorites, and saved routes are stored locally on your device only.

2. Location Data

The App's core purpose is to let you simulate a GPS location. Important points:

  • The mock location you choose is stored locally on your device and is never uploaded to any server.
  • Core location spoofing works fully offline — no internet connection is required.
  • The App does not track your real location, movement history, or route history.
  • The App does not share location data with third parties.

3. Permissions We Use

  • Location (mock location / ACCESS_MOCK_LOCATION) — required to provide the fake GPS feature. Only active when you explicitly enable Mock Location in Android Developer Options and start spoofing from the App.
  • Internet — used only to load map tiles and, optionally, for anonymous analytics as described below.
  • Storage (local) — used to save your preferences, favorites, and routes on your device.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, which may collect anonymous, non-personal information:

  • Google Firebase Analytics — collects anonymous usage statistics (e.g., screens viewed, app version, device model) to help us improve the App. No personal or location data is included.
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics — collects crash reports (device type, OS version, stack traces) so we can fix bugs. No personal or location data is included.
  • Map tiles (MapLibre / OpenStreetMap-based providers) — map images are loaded from public tile servers when you browse the map. No personal data is sent to these servers.

You can learn more about how Google handles data at: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Data Security

Since the App does not collect or store your personal data on any server, there is no personal data at risk. Everything you create in the App stays on your device. You can remove all App data at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing its data in Android Settings.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will address it.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
