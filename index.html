Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

Effective date: October 17, 2026

Mock Shift is built for developer testing, debugging, and verifying location-based applications.

Data collection

Mock Shift collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no tracking of any kind.

Where your data lives

Everything you create — saved places, saved routes, favorites, history, settings, and downloaded routing data — is stored only on your device. Nothing is uploaded to any server, and nothing leaves the app except the mock GPS coordinates you explicitly choose to broadcast to other apps via Android's mock-location mechanism.

Network usage

The app uses the internet only to display the map and to download optional offline routing data (OpenStreetMap-based tiles) when you request it. Map requests go to OpenStreetMap's public tile servers; routing data downloads go to brouter.de (or its mirror). No user-identifying information is sent with these requests.

Permissions

  • Location access: required to provide mock locations to other apps.
  • Notifications: required by Android to show the persistent Fake GPS status while spoofing is active.

Third parties

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License (ODbL). Routing engine: BRouter (MIT license).

Your data rights

This app has no backend and stores nothing about you, so there is no user data to access, correct, or delete beyond what you can already manage in the app itself (Clear history / Clear saved data in Settings).
